How it works

1. Extract

Reads TXT, HTML, DOCX and PDF and separates hidden content from visible text.

2. Detect

Rules find injection phrases, role overrides, exfiltration and PII, with a reason for each.

3. Decide

The risk score (0-100) picks allow, sanitize or quarantine.

4. Sanitize

Injected sentences are removed and PII is redacted, then the result is re-scanned.

5. Index

Only safe content enters the vector database the RAG reads from.
